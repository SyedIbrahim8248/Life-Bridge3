🌱 Our Mission / हमारा उद्देश्य

🆘 Important Safety Notice / जरूरी सुरक्षा सूचना

LifeBridge is for general mentorship, education, conversation and personal support. It is not a replacement for qualified medical or mental-health care.

LifeBridge सामान्य mentorship, education और supportive conversation के लिए है। यह professional medical या mental-health treatment का replacement नहीं है।

If you or someone else is in immediate danger or at risk of self-harm, contact your local emergency service or qualified professional immediately.

अगर तुरंत खतरा हो या self-harm का risk हो तो local emergency service या qualified professional से तुरंत संपर्क करें।
